Questions teams ask first.

Do users leave my app to deposit?

No. Deposits live inside your UI with no redirects, on web and mobile.

What happens when a bridge stalls?

Unifold skips it and routes the deposit through another bridge. A weak geo or a stalled bridge reroutes with no code change.

How much code does the integration take?

About ten lines with one SDK. It replaces a separate integration for each ramp and each bridge.

Which SDKs and platforms are supported?

React, React Native, Swift, Kotlin, Flutter and Svelte, with native experiences on iOS and Android.

Which payment methods can my users use?

Bank deposits and withdrawals, Cash App, Apple Pay, card, wallets and exchange accounts, in any token on any chain.

Can I keep full control of the interface?

Yes. The headless option runs the ramp flow through your own interface.

What if I need something that is not supported yet?

Tell us what you are scoping. We will scope it out with you and integrate it into the widget.
